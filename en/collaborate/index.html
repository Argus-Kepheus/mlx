Collaborate

The Multilinguistics project welcomes the collaboration of researchers interested in the linguistic transformations associated with the digital era. Participation may take place in any of the research fronts and may take the form of co-authorship of studies and articles, specialist review, or specific contributions such as the selection and analysis of data.

Profiles sought

  • Sociolinguistics, variation and language contact, particularly among Spanish, Portuguese and English.
  • Corpus linguistics and computational linguistics, including the study of texts produced or mediated by artificial intelligence.
  • Writing, register and norm in digital environments.
  • Translation studies and machine translation with large language models.
  • Applied linguistics and technology-mediated language teaching.
  • Indigenous languages in digital environments, for the project's future stage.

The scope of the project may be widened or redefined according to the interests and participation of its collaborators.

Working method

Collaboration is coordinated by the editor and takes place through delimited tasks communicated by e-mail. Each task states what is requested, the materials supplied, the format of the response and the deadline. The results received are incorporated into the project and may give rise to scientific articles or to content on this site.

Credit

Contributions are recognised according to the CRediT taxonomy (Contributor Roles Taxonomy). Each participant's roles are assigned on the basis of the tasks carried out and submitted to the co-authors for confirmation before each article is submitted. Contributions that do not amount to authorship are recorded in the acknowledgements.

Contact

Contact: sergiocc@id.uff.br
