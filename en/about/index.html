About

Organisation

The Multilinguistics project is maintained by Editora Argus Kepheus, a Brazilian publisher.

Participation

The research is carried out by co-authors and collaborators whose participation is coordinated by the editor; the names of the participants are given in each study and each publication.

Methods and editorial policy

  • Methods: how claims are grounded, checked and reviewed.
  • Editorial policy: approval of content, contributions and corrections.
  • Methodological guide: questions and answers on the procedures of the reviews, with their justifications.
  • Glossary: meaning of the terms used by the project.

Contact and corrections

Contact: sergiocc@id.uff.br

Corrections and comments on the content of the site may be sent to the same address, indicating the page and the passage they concern.
