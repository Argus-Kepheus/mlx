Editorial policy

Exception to the approval and prior human-review rules below: translations explicitly labelled provisional are published with the editor's authorisation before human linguistic review. This applies to the Southern Bolivian Quechua version; the approved Spanish source prevails in case of discrepancy.

Roles and responsibilities

Approval and updates

Each page of this site is approved by the editor, in each language, before it is published. The content follows the development of the project: every update is submitted for approval again, and the date of the last update appears in the footer of each page.

Sources and verification

Claims rest on recorded sources, checked as described in Methods.

Contributions

Material from co-authors and collaborators reaches the project through the editor, who coordinates the tasks, decides on its incorporation and records contributions according to the CRediT taxonomy. Screeners and reviewers of Argus Kepheus Publishing House who work anonymously, as in peer review, are not identified. This site does not receive submissions or confidential reviews.

Publications

Each published article has its own page, with the full reference, the journal (with its ISSN and website), the full text when there is a licence to reproduce it, and the associated data.

Corrections

Errors and comments may be reported to the editor at sergiocc@id.uff.br, indicating the page and the passage they concern. The corrected page then shows the new date of its last update.
