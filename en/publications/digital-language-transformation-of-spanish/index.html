Lenguaje digital y transformación del español: una revisión crítica del estado del arte

published review article

Calvimontes Calvimontes, T. de J. (2026). Lenguaje digital y transformación del español: una revisión crítica del estado del arte [Digital language and the transformation of Spanish: a critical review of the state of the art]. Revista Científica Humanidades, 8(13), 83–96.

The article critically reviews the literature on the transformations of Spanish in digital environments, with attention to linguistic economy, multimodality and sociolinguistic variation. It is written in Spanish and is a publication related to the Digital multilingualism front.
